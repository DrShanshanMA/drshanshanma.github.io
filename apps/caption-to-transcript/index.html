Caption → Transcript Converter

Convert caption files into clean Word transcripts. Upload one or several files, remove caption numbering, keep timestamps at the beginning of each paragraph, and download editable .docx documents.

Select your caption documents
Supported: TXT, SRT, VTT, SBV, SUB. Files are processed in this browser; their text is not uploaded to a server.
    What the converter does
    • Removes caption sequence numbers and timing ranges.
    • Preserves the spoken text and, optionally, adds the starting timestamp to each paragraph.
    • Creates an editable Word document with a title and readable paragraph spacing.
    • Supports multiple files in one batch; each file gets its own transcript.

    Note: creating Word files requires an internet connection to load the document-generation library. Your caption text is processed locally in this browser.

    For best results, use the original caption export. Automatic captions may contain recognition errors; this tool does not verify the spoken words.
