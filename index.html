About & Experience

Thoughtful design starts with learning.

I work with instructors and teams to translate teaching goals into clear, engaging learning experiences.

My background spans instructional design, teaching, and research in learning technologies. I hold a Ph.D. in Learning Technologies from the University of North Texas and work in digital learning at California Lutheran University.

I’m particularly interested in how AI can support educators and learners while keeping instructional purpose, accessibility, and human expertise at the center of the design process.

Research & Publications

Curiosity grounded in evidence.

My research explores the relationship between learning, instructional design, and technology.

My interests include instructional designers’ AI competencies and institutional readiness, as well as how learners experience AI-generated instructional video. My research has appeared in journals including Learning and Instruction, the British Journal of Educational Technology, and TechTrends.

I have also taught courses in learning technologies and directed research, bringing a researcher’s curiosity and a practitioner’s perspective to teaching.
